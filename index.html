Fresh from our partner farms

A diversified agribusiness, registered with CAC Nigeria

We serve farmers, businesses and international buyers through sustainable farming, practical agricultural solutions and efficient supply chain management. Our work spans crop production, livestock, poultry, fisheries, fertilizer production, consultancy, mechanized farming, and the import and export of agricultural products.

We are a corporate member of the Federation of Agricultural Commodities Association of Nigeria.

Our products are certified with NAFDAC, SON, NSQ and other regulatory bodies and agencies in Nigeria.
